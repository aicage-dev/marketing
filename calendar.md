# Posting Calendar

## Pre-launch checklist

- [x] Create Hacker News account
- [x] Create Reddit account(s)
- [ ] Gather screenshot of TUI overview screen
- [ ] Record GIF of `aicage claude` starting (optional but high value)
- [ ] Review and finalize all post drafts

## Week 1: Account warm-up

Before posting promotional content, establish that your accounts are real:

- **Day 1-2:** Create accounts on HN, Reddit
- **Day 3-5:** Make a few genuine comments on other posts in target subreddits
- **Day 5-7:** Upvote and engage with relevant content (Docker, AI tools, coding agents)

## Week 2: Launch

### Tuesday – Hacker News
- Post Show HN between 9-11am US Eastern
- Monitor and reply to comments for the first 4 hours
- Best days: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday

### Thursday – Reddit r/docker
- Post the Docker-focused draft
- Reply to comments

### Friday – Reddit r/programming
- Post the programming-focused draft
- Reply to comments

### Saturday – Reddit r/LocalLLaMA
- Post the LocalLLaMA-focused draft
- Reply to comments

## Week 3: Follow-up

- Monitor all posts for new comments/questions
- Respond to every comment within 24 hours
- If HN made it to front page: prepare a follow-up comment with technical details
- Note any feature requests or common questions for future content

## Week 4: Sustained content

- Write a follow-up post based on community feedback
- Consider a "what I learned from launching" post
- Start planning regular update posts for new releases

## Ongoing

- Release announcements for new versions
- Feature highlight posts when adding notable features
- Community engagement (reply to issues, discussions)
- Monthly check-in: review metrics, adjust strategy

## Timing notes

- **HN peak:** Tue-Thu 9am-12pm US Eastern
- **Reddit peak:** Tue-Thu 10am-2pm US Eastern
- Avoid weekends for HN (lower traffic)
- Reddit r/programming is less time-sensitive
